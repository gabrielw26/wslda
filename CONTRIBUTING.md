# Contributing to W-SLDA

## For scientists

Scientific research is driven by exploring new questions and pushing existing methods beyond their current limits. While we strive to make the W-SLDA Toolkit as flexible and general as possible, there may be cases where the current implementation does not fully address your specific research problem. If you encounter such a limitation, we strongly encourage you to contact us. We will be happy to discuss your use case and help identify the relevant parts of the code that may require extension or modification.

## For computer scientists and students

We warmly invite computer scientists, software engineers, and students interested in high-performance computing and scientific software development to contribute to the W-SLDA Toolkit or related projects.

If you would like to get involved, please contact the W-SLDA Team at:  
**wslda@fizyka.pw.edu.pl**
