What is the difference between a physicist, an engineer, and a mathematician?
- If an engineer walks into a room and sees a fire in the middle and a bucket of water in the corner, 
  he takes the bucket of water and pours it on the fire and puts it out.
- If a physicist walks into a room and sees a fire in the middle and a bucket of water in the corner, 
  he takes the bucket of water and pours it eloquently around the fire and lets the fire put itself out.
- If a mathematician walks into a room and sees a fire in the middle and a bucket of water in the corner, 
  he convinces himself there is a solution and leaves. (credit: Jeremiah Jazdzewski)
